A synthetic flow for the kit's selftest: every parser case Atlas has, with the warnings it gives.
Its golden files come from Atlas's own parser (`scripts/flow-golden.ts` in the workbench).
It is a `.txt`, not a `.md`: three of its lines are not valid YAML on purpose, and the workbench's
ingest reads every `.md` under `skills/` as YAML and stops on the first it cannot parse.
